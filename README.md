# Photowalk Bingo: Astoria Park

Interactive bingo card for the Pexels photowalk at Astoria Park (Nov 7, 2026).

- Tap a square to add a photo and mark it done.
- The yellow squares are your own ideas.
- Photos stay in your own browser (IndexedDB). Nothing is uploaded.
- "Save my card as an image" and "Download my photos" keep a copy outside the browser.

Printable versions: `pexels-photowalk-bingo-7x7.pdf` (color) and `pexels-photowalk-bingo-7x7-bw.pdf` (black and white).

## Publish with GitHub Pages
Settings > Pages > Deploy from a branch > `main` / root.
